# FPV display references — 1 November 2026

Research scope: screen presentation for the fictional arcade game.

## Sources and access limits
- [Bellingcat, Identifying Small Drones from Screenshots and Displays](https://www.bellingcat.com/resources/2024/01/19/identifying-small-drones-from-screenshots-and-displays/) (19 January 2024): inspected its written visual analysis and captions. It documents on-screen telemetry, battery warnings, wide-angle imagery and video noise.
- Its linked [OSINTtechnical X post](https://twitter.com/Osinttechnical/status/1666255898208088064) and [Serhii Sternenko X post](https://twitter.com/sternenko/status/1735284315179643295) returned HTTP 403. Direct X search also produced sparse results; the accessible page for another indexed post had no readable content. No X video was successfully played or authenticated.
- The requested heart reticle is an original SVG drawn for this game. Research did not establish its provenance or prevalence in Ukrainian footage.

## Applied
- Clear battery charge, countdown and low/critical text warnings.
- Compact elapsed-flight OSD timer.
- Brief static-styled power-loss handoff to recon.
- Heart outline with an exact centre aiming dot, visible in both sensor modes.

The 60-second battery, 1.8× boost drain, warning thresholds and sound cues are game design decisions, not claims about real equipment. No third-party footage, logos or screenshots were imported.

## Possible later presentation work
- Optional subtle video grain and a wider FPV field of view, with readability/motion-comfort settings.
- A brief signal-interruption effect on damage, without obscuring controls or target visibility.
- Stronger visual distinction between the stable recon feed and the FPV feed.

These are presentation proposals, not verified features of the inaccessible X videos. Existing responsive arcade controls stay central.
